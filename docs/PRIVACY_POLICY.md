# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-19

This Privacy Policy explains how EmoLor collects, uses, stores, and protects information in the mobile application and related services.

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use EmoLor:

- Account information such as email address, password, full name, phone number, account type, and role.
- Caregiver and family profile information, including child profile names, dates of birth, avatar images, and related profile metadata.
- Parent/caregiver PIN or credential data used for child access and account security.
- Emotional and activity data captured during app use, including:
  - pre-session and post-session emotion names
  - mood/valence values
  - chosen colours and sensory zone values
  - activity session timing and completion metadata
  - regulation delta and mismatch analytics used for caregiver reporting
- App usage and reporting information needed to generate weekly insights and PDF summaries.

Some information may be stored locally on the device and synchronised to the backend service used by the app.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use the information collected to:

- create and manage user accounts
- personalise the caregiver and child experience in the app
- create and maintain child profiles and family links
- track child emotional progress during sessions
- generate caregiver analytics, weekly summaries, and PDF reports
- support authentication, account recovery, and security protections
- improve the quality and reliability of the service

## 3. Data Storage and Processing

EMOLOR uses a Supabase backend for authentication, profile storage, child session data, and related app records. Data may also be processed by third-party service providers necessary to operate the product, such as:

- cloud hosting and database services
- AI processing for caregiver insight summaries
- PDF generation and sharing features

We do not sell personal data. Information is used only for the operation of the app and the services described in this policy.

## 4. Children’s Data

The app may collect information about children for the purpose of emotional learning and caregiver monitoring. This information is intended to support parent/caregiver oversight and is not used for marketing or unrelated profiling.

Caregivers are responsible for controlling access to child profiles and associated data within the app.

## 5. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organisational safeguards to protect personal information, including secure authentication, access controls, and backend protections. No system is completely immune to risk, but we aim to minimise unauthorised access and misuse.

## 6. Retention

We retain account and profile data for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain account records, support legal obligations, and provide analytics and reports. If an account is deactivated or a profile is removed, we may retain limited records where necessary for operational, legal, or safety reasons.

## 7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to:

- access the personal data we hold about you
- correct inaccurate information
- request deletion or deactivation of your account
- restrict or object to certain processing activities
- request information about how your data is used

To request account or data deletion, use the [EmoLor account and data deletion page](https://thanesh6.github.io/EmoLor/data-deletion.html). You can also contact the app administrator or support contact for your organisation if you cannot access the app.

The deletion page explains the in-app steps, the data removed or made inaccessible, and the limited records that may be retained for up to 90 days or longer where required by law or a documented safety obligation.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If changes are material, we will notify users through the app or other reasonable means before the update becomes effective.

## 9. Contact

For privacy questions, account requests, or data concerns, please contact the relevant product or administrator contact for your organisation.

This policy is intended to describe the app’s data practices accurately based on the current system design and should be reviewed alongside any local legal requirements and platform policies.
