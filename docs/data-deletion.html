EmoLor account and data deletion

Last updated: 19 September 2026

Request account deletion

To request deletion of your EmoLor account and associated data, follow these steps:

  1. Sign in to the EmoLor mobile app.
  2. Open your profile or account settings.
  3. Select Deactivate Account.
  4. Enter your account password and confirm the request.

The app signs you out immediately and disables the account. If you cannot sign in or cannot complete these steps, contact the administrator or support contact for the organisation that provided your EmoLor account and ask for an EmoLor account and data deletion request.

What is deleted

  • Your account is deactivated and removed from active use.
  • Your account profile is hidden from other users.
  • Local app data and saved session data are cleared from the device when the in-app request is completed.
  • Child profiles and family links that belong exclusively to the account are removed or made inaccessible according to the organisation's account-management controls.

What may be retained

EmoLor may retain limited account, audit, security, billing, or safety records when necessary to operate the service, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or meet legal obligations. Data retained for these purposes is restricted and is deleted when the applicable purpose or legal retention requirement ends. Deactivation is processed immediately; retained records are reviewed and removed within 90 days unless a longer period is required by law or a documented safety obligation.

Delete selected data without deleting the account

Users can remove individual child profiles from the account-management area where that feature is available. This removes the selected profile or family link but does not delete the caregiver account. For any other specific data request, contact the administrator or support contact for your organisation and identify the data you want removed.

Privacy information

For the full data collection, use, sharing, and retention details, read the EmoLor Privacy Policy.

EmoLor is the name of the application covered by this request page.
